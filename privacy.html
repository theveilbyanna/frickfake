Privacy Policy

readme.txt · the short version
  • We collect what you give us (email, screen name, profile, photo, posts, messages) plus the little bit of technical info needed to keep you logged in and keep the site running.
  • We use it to run FrickFake, verify members and keep people safe. That’s it.

The short version is here to help you. The full text below is what actually counts.

1.Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how FrickFake, a Digital Outlaw project based in California, USA (“FrickFake,” “we,” “us”), collects, uses and protects information about you when you use frickfake.online, the FrickFake app, the waitlist and the suggestion box (the “Service”). It is part of our Terms of Service.

2.What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Your first name or screen name and your email address
  • Your city and your answer to “What made you quit fake social media?”, if you choose to give them
  • How you found us (a referral code in the link, if there was one), the date and time you joined, your FrickFake number, and the fact that you checked the agreement box

When you create an account and use the app

  • Account details: screen name, email address and password. We store your password only as a one-way scrambled “hash,” so we can’t see it.
  • Profile: city, interests, bio, profile photo, status and away message, theme and colors, profile song link and Top 8, if you add them
  • Activity: buddy requests and buddies, blocks, messages (what they say, when they were sent and read), posts and “real” reactions, reports you make or that others make about you, suggestion-box ideas and votes, and when you were last active (so your buddies see you online)
  • Agreement and security records: when you agreed to our Terms, your FrickFake number, failed login attempts and temporary lockouts

Automatically

  • Technical info: like every website, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address, browser and device type, and basic request logs to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We don’t use them to build a profile of you.
  • One login cookie and a couple of small settings saved in your browser (see section 5).

When you contact us

  • Whatever you send us, like emails, password reset requests, reports and appeals.

What we don’t collect: your precise location, your contacts or address book, your phone number, government ID, or payment information.

3.How we use it

  • To run FrickFake: give you a number, set up your account and profile, and make buddy lists, chat, posts, search and the suggestion box work
  • To verify members and keep bots, scammers and fake accounts out. A real person makes verification decisions, not an algorithm
  • For safety: review reports, enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and protect members and the public
  • For security: keep you logged in, block password-guessing and stop abuse
  • To talk to you about FrickFake: launch news for the waitlist, important account and service messages, and replies when you write to us. You can ask us to stop non-essential emails anytime
  • To improve FrickFake, for example by reading suggestion-box ideas
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests

We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t sell it.

4.Who can see your info

  • Other verified members can see your screen name, number, profile (photo, city, interests, bio, status, away message, theme, profile song, Top 8 and the month you joined), and whether you’re online. Members can find you in search by your screen name, number, city, interests or bio. Photos are only shown to signed-in members.
  • Your buddies can see your posts. Posts show only to your accepted buddies.
  • Messages are visible to you and the person you’re chatting with. They are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • FrickFake moderators (right now, that’s the founder) can see sign-up details (including your email, city, why you joined and your photo) to verify you, and reports. We access other information, including messages, only when needed to investigate a report, keep someone safe, secure the Service or follow the law.
  • Service providers that run FrickFake for us and may only use your info to provide their services: Vercel (website hosting and file storage) and Neon (database). Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to get them.
  • Music players: if a profile has a song, the player is loaded from Spotify, YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) or SoundCloud when you view it. Those companies may collect information and set cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if the law requires it (for example, a subpoena or court order), to protect someone’s safety, or to investigate fraud or abuse. We report child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • If FrickFake changes hands: if FrickFake is sold, merged or reorganized, your information may transfer as part of that, and this policy will still protect it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising (that’s California’s term for ad tracking).

5.Cookies and tracking

  • One login cookie keeps you signed in. It’s strictly necessary, and it lasts up to 90 days or until you log out.
  • Browser storage: the app saves a couple of small settings on your device (for example, remembering that you’ve signed up before, so it shows the login screen).
  • No trackers: we don’t use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • Because we don’t sell or share your info, there’s nothing to opt out of. We still respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals.

6.How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account.

Deleting your account: go to Me → Delete account in the app. That permanently erases your account, profile, photo, buddies, blocks, posts, reactions, your messages (both sides of every conversation you were in) and your waitlist sign-up. Ideas you posted in the suggestion box and reports you filed may stay, but without your name attached.

Waitlist only (no account)? Email us to have your sign-up deleted.

Some copies may stay for a limited time in backups and logs kept by our service providers until they’re overwritten. We may keep information longer if the law requires it, to resolve disputes, or for banned accounts, to keep the person from coming back.

7.Your choices

  • Edit your profile or remove your photo anytime in the app.
  • Block anyone, and choose who becomes your buddy.
  • Delete your account anytime.
  • Email hello@frickfake.online to get a copy of your information, fix something that’s wrong, delete your info, or stop non-essential emails.

8.Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) gives you rights over your personal information. We honor these rights for every member, wherever they live.

  • Right to know what personal information we’ve collected about you, where it came from, why we use it and who we disclose it to, and to get a copy of it.
  • Right to delete your personal information, with limited exceptions.
  • Right to correct information that’s wrong.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We don’t sell or share personal information, so there’s nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. The only sensitive information we have is your login details and the contents of your messages, and we use them only to provide the Service, so this right doesn’t come up.
  • Right not to be discriminated against for using any of these rights.

How to make a request: email hello@frickfake.online with “Privacy request” in the subject. To protect you, we’ll confirm it’s you by matching the request to the email on your account and may ask you to confirm from that address. You can have an authorized agent make a request for you if you give them signed permission and confirm your identity with us. We’ll respond within 45 days, as the law requires.

What we’ve collected in the last 12 months

  • Identifiers: name or screen name, email address, FrickFake number, IP address
  • Customer records: name, email and photo
  • Internet or other network activity: how you use the Service, such as buddies, posts, reactions and last-active time
  • General location: the city you type in, if any (never precise location)
  • Audio, electronic or visual information: your profile photo
  • Sensitive personal information: account login details and the contents of your messages

We collect these from you, from your device, and from other members (for example, when they message you or report an account). We use them for the purposes in section 3, and we disclose them only as described in section 4. We haven’t sold or shared any of them.

“Shine the Light.” We don’t share personal information with other companies for their own direct marketing.

9.Adults only

FrickFake is only for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has signed up, we delete their account and information. If you think that’s happened, email hello@frickfake.online.

10.How we protect your info

We use HTTPS encryption on every page, store passwords only as hashes, keep the login cookie locked down (HttpOnly and Secure), slow down repeated login guesses, and protect moderator tools with a secret key. No system is 100% secure, though, so we can’t guarantee your information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects your information, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

11.Where your info is stored

FrickFake is run from the United States, and your information is stored and processed there by us and our service providers. If you use FrickFake from outside the U.S., you understand that your information will be transferred to the U.S., where privacy laws may be different from those where you live.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We’ll post the new version here with a new effective date, and if a change is significant, we’ll also let you know on the site or in the app before it takes effect.

13.Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@frickfake.online. FrickFake, a Digital Outlaw project, California, USA.
