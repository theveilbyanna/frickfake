Copyright & Takedowns

Effective November 22, 2026

readme.txt · the short version

The short version is here to help you. The full text below is what actually counts.

FrickFake respects the rights of creators and expects members to do the same. We respond to notices of claimed copyright infringement that follow the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512.

1.Report copyright infringement

If you believe something on FrickFake infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to represent, send our Copyright Agent a written notice that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature (typing your full legal name works);
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you believe was infringed;
  3. a description of the material you want removed and where it is on FrickFake, detailed enough for us to find it (for example, the member’s screen name or number, and which photo, post or profile field);
  4. your name, mailing address, phone number and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and
  6. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner’s behalf.

Send notices to:
Copyright Agent, FrickFake
Email: hello@frickfake.online (subject: “Copyright notice”)

When we get a complete notice, we’ll remove or disable access to the material and let the member who posted it know.

2.Send a counter-notice

If your content was removed because of a copyright notice and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to the same address. It must include:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. a description of the material that was removed and where it appeared before it was removed;
  3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification;
  4. your name, mailing address and phone number; and
  5. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district where your address is located (or, if your address is outside the U.S., any judicial district in which FrickFake may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice, or their agent.

When we get a valid counter-notice, we’ll send a copy to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us they’ve filed a court action to keep the material down, we may restore it in 10 to 14 business days.

3.Repeat infringers

We will, in appropriate circumstances, disable or terminate the accounts of members who repeatedly infringe copyrights.

4.Be accurate

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages. If you’re not sure whether something infringes your rights, consider talking to a lawyer first.

5.Trademarks and other complaints

For trademark concerns, impersonation or other intellectual property issues, email hello@frickfake.online with the details and we’ll look into it.
