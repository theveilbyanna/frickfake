Disclaimer

“Verified” is not a background check. When we verify a new member, a real person at FrickFake has reviewed their sign-up and decided to let them in. That helps keep out bots and obvious fakes. You’re responsible for your own safety. Never send money, gift cards or crypto to anyone you meet here. Keep your passwords and financial info to yourself. If you meet someone in person, meet in a public place and tell someone you trust where you’ll be.
