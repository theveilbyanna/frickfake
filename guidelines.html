Community Guidelines

Effective November 22, 2026

readme.txt · the short version
  • Be real. One account, your own identity, no bots, no fakes.
  • Be decent. No harassment, hate, threats or scams.
  • Respect “Nope.” If someone says no or blocks you, leave them alone.
  • Report what’s wrong. A real person reads every report.

The short version is here to help you. The full text below is what actually counts.

FrickFake exists because the big platforms filled up with bots, scammers and jerks. These rules keep this place for real people. They’re part of our Terms of Service, and they apply everywhere on FrickFake: profiles, photos, posts, messages, statuses, away messages, screen names and the suggestion box.

1.Be the real you

  • One account per person, in your own identity. No fake accounts, duplicate accounts, bots or automated accounts.
  • Don’t pretend to be someone else, including other members, public figures or FrickFake staff.
  • The photo you use for verification has to be you.
  • Don’t sell, share or hand off your account.

2.No scams, ever

  • Never ask members for money, gift cards, crypto, loans, bank or card details, or “investments.”
  • No romance scams, phishing links, fake giveaways, pyramid schemes or get-rich-quick pitches.
  • No selling, advertising or affiliate links unless we say it’s OK.

3.No harassment or bullying

  • No threats, intimidation, stalking or repeated unwanted contact.
  • No doxxing: don’t post anyone’s private info, like their address, phone number, workplace or private photos.
  • Don’t encourage anyone to hurt themselves.
  • No dogpiling or getting others to pile on someone.
  • Don’t punish anyone for blocking or reporting you.

4.Respect “Nope”

Nobody owes you a buddy request. If someone hits “Nope,” removes you or blocks you, accept it. Don’t make new accounts, use friends or post about them to get around it.

5.No hate

Don’t attack, threaten or demean people because of their race, ethnicity, national origin, caste, religion, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, serious illness or age. Don’t use slurs against people, and don’t promote hate groups or hate symbols.

6.No violence or dangerous stuff

  • No threats of violence, and no praising or encouraging violent attacks or terrorism.
  • No selling or arranging weapons, drugs or other illegal goods or services.
  • No instructions or encouragement for hurting people or animals.

7.Adults only, and no explicit content

  • Anything that sexualizes a minor gets the account banned and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement. No exceptions.
  • FrickFake is 18+ only. Don’t sign up if you’re under 18, and report anyone you think is.
  • No nudity, pornography or sexually explicit images or videos anywhere on FrickFake, including messages.
  • Never share intimate images of anyone without their consent.
  • No sexual harassment or unwanted sexual comments.

8.No spam

No mass buddy requests, copy-paste messages, chain letters, repeated posts or anything designed to game FrickFake numbers, votes or reactions.

9.Respect privacy and other people’s work

  • Don’t share private conversations or other members’ info to embarrass or target them.
  • Don’t collect, scrape or record members’ info.
  • Only post photos, words and other content you have the right to share. See Copyright & Takedowns.

10.Don’t break FrickFake

No hacking, scraping, bots, overloading the site or getting around bans and limits. If you find a bug or security hole, tell us at hello@frickfake.online instead of using it.

11.Follow the law

Don’t use FrickFake for anything illegal, or to plan or promote anything illegal.

12.How to report

  • In the app: open the member’s profile and tap Report. You can also block them so they can’t find, add or message you.
  • By email: hello@frickfake.online, for anything urgent or anything you can’t report in the app.
  • In an emergency or if someone is in danger, call 911. If someone is thinking about suicide or self-harm, they can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US).

13.What happens when rules are broken

A real person reviews reports. Depending on what happened, we may warn the member, remove content, limit features, take away verification, suspend the account or ban it for good. Serious cases (threats, child safety, scams) may go straight to a ban and be reported to the authorities. We consider context, but we decide what’s acceptable.

Think we made a mistake? Email hello@frickfake.online within 30 days and we’ll take another look.

14.Stay safe out there

  • Never send money, gift cards or crypto to someone you met online, no matter the story.
  • Keep your address, workplace, passwords and financial info to yourself.
  • “Verified” means a real person let them in. It’s not a background check.
  • Meeting up? Pick a public place, get there on your own, and tell someone you trust.
  • Trust your gut. Block and report anything that feels off.
